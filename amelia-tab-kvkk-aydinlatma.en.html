Amelia Tab — KVKK Information Notice

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This notice is provided to users of the Amelia Tab browser extension under Article 10 of Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). For which data is sent by which feature, and for the extension's permissions, see the Privacy Policy. The Turkish version is the original text; if the two differ, the Turkish version prevails.

1. Data controller

  • Data controller: Umut İlyas
  • Contact: fatal.iv@protonmail.com

2. Personal data processed

Only when the relevant feature is used: location or city information, AI chat messages and API key, domain names of sites, the background search term and the IP address. The detailed table is in section 3 of the Privacy Policy.

3. Purpose and legal basis

  • Purpose: Making the feature chosen by the user work (weather, AI chat, background, sound, site icons) and protecting the service against abuse.
  • Legal basis: For location data, the browser's permission prompt and the user's consent; for other data, the controller's legitimate interest (KVKK art. 5/2-f) in providing the chosen feature and keeping the service secure. Data is collected when the relevant feature is used, through that feature's interface and by automated means.

4. Recipients and transfers abroad

Depending on the feature used, data may be passed to the following groups of recipients:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: the cloud infrastructure provider hosting the Amelia Tab service server.
  • Services providing the feature: weather (OpenWeatherMap), background photos (Unsplash), white noise (Freesound) and site icons (Google).
  • AI providers: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google (Gemini) and DeepSeek. Data is passed only to the provider the user selects; DeepSeek is a China-based provider.

Because these services' servers are located abroad, using the relevant feature involves a transfer of data abroad.

  • The user chooses the AI provider and sends the message; selecting a provider and sending a message is treated as the user's choice to have the data passed to that provider.
  • AI chat content is subject to the selected provider's own privacy policy. Sharing sensitive or personal information in chat is not recommended.
  • Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared for purposes such as credit or scoring.

5. Rights of the data subject (KVKK art. 11)

By applying to the data controller, the data subject has the right to:

  • learn whether their personal data is processed,
  • request information if it has been processed,
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used in line with that purpose,
  • know the third parties to whom data is transferred at home or abroad,
  • request correction of incomplete or incorrectly processed data,
  • request deletion or destruction of data, and that these actions be notified to third parties to whom data was transferred,
  • object to an unfavourable result arising from analysis of the data exclusively by automated systems,
  • claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

How to apply: Applications are sent to fatal.iv@protonmail.com with enough information to verify identity, and are concluded within 30 days at most, depending on the nature of the request. Because the extension has no accounts, secrets such as API keys must not be shared in an application.
